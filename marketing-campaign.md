---

## 1. Positioning recap (keep every ad on-brand)

- **Who:** SMBs & micro-businesses in Spain (restaurants, clinics, shops, landlords). Also norms/boomers — non-technical owners.
- **What:** "Hire a human, get an AI that works." We set it up FOR you (concierge), not a self-serve SaaS.
- **Channels:** WhatsApp (included) + web chat + email.
- **Price anchor:** public tiers Free / Starter $29 / Business $79 / Pro custom — but the *conversation* sells the tailored plan.
- **Differentiator vs pure SaaS:** a real person configures & stays with you (1:1 call, group onboarding, open-doors dates, tutorials).
- **Core message:** "Your business answers 24/7 without hiring anyone. We set it up for you."

## 2. Channel plan (Phase 1, low budget ~€300–500/mo)

| # | Channel | Daily budget | Best for | Verdict |
|---|---------|--------------|----------|---------|
| 1 | **Google Search Ads** (exact match) | €10–15 | High-intent ("agente IA WhatsApp negocio") | ⭐ Best ROI, start here |

### Kill rule
Track **cost per lead**; pause any channel that stays > **€25/lead** after 2 weeks.

## 3. Google Search Ads

**Campaign:** Search, exact match, ES geo.

**Keywords (Phase 1 — 3 ad groups):**
1. `agente ia whatsapp negocio`
2. `agente ia para empresas`
3. `automatizar whatsapp con ia`
(Add long-tail negatives: "gratis curso", "whatsapp web", "bot tutorial".)

**Ad copy (ES):**
- Headline 1: `Agente IA para tu negocio 24/7`
- Headline 2: `Te lo montamos nosotros`
- Description: `Atiende WhatsApp, web y email automáticamente. Sin instalar nada, sin contratar. Configuración en 24h.`

**Landing:** `/` → CTA to `/try` or contact form. Add UTM params per ad group.

## 4. Meta Ads

**Objective:** Leads → contact (WhatsApp / form).

**Creative 1 — video demo (15–30s):** a WhatsApp conversation auto-answered by the agent (price inquiry → booking). Caption: "Tu negocio responde solo. 24/7."

**Creative 2 — carousel "3 formas de automatizar tu negocio":**
1. Responde WhatsApp mientras duermes
2. Gestiona reservas y pedidos solos
3. Te mandamos un resumen diario de todo

**Geo:** Spain. **Audience:** small business owners, 25-55.
